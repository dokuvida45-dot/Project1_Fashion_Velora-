The House of Velora

A studio-curated boutique committed to craft, longevity and quiet luxury.

Our origin

Velora Couture was founded in 2018 in a small Parisian studio by three friends with a shared frustration: that "luxury" had become a synonym for branded mass production. We set out to build a different kind of fashion house — one where every garment carried the name of the person who made it, where every fabric had a verifiable origin, and where every customer was treated like a guest in our atelier.

Slow fashion, properly considered

We release only two collections a year. Every piece is produced in small batches by ateliers in France, Italy and Portugal. We use natural fibres certified by the Global Organic Textile Standard, and we publish a transparent breakdown of where your money goes — from fibre to finishing.

Responsibility

Our supply chain is audited annually by an independent body. We pay above-living wages, we offset our shipping emissions, and we operate a lifetime repair guarantee on all leather goods. Garments returned at end-of-life are remade or recycled through our circular programme.

Our team

Velora is a team of 24 people across Paris, Florence and London. Our buyers travel for half the year to meet the makers we partner with. Our concierge team is trained in personal styling and offers consultations by appointment, in person or online.

Accessibility statement

We are committed to digital accessibility. Our website is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards, and we welcome feedback at accessibility@velora.example. We offer a screen-reader friendly catalogue, transcripts for all video content, and keyboard navigation throughout.
